Collection objects · provisional associations

Scan a session folder to build coordinate-based tracks.

Positions within 3 arcseconds of a running centre can join a track only when the match is unambiguous in that exposure. Nearby competing detections are left unassigned. 4. Browse the light-curve gallery

Open or close the gallery from this heading, its bottom button, or above the main chart. Selecting a tile opens its full curve in section 5; the switch below can also close the gallery. Back to gallery returns to the tile you selected.

Uses the star search and minimum-observation filters above. Each thumbnail shows unfiltered 5-minute medians, centred on the star's median, over the same session timeline. Fainter is lower. Gaps over 15 minutes are disconnected. Click a curve to inspect it.

Review priority is robust binned range relative to scatter, reduced for sparse coverage. It is not an eclipse probability or a matching-quality score. Track fragments and instrumental effects can rank highly. Auto-scale can exaggerate small changes.

How variability screening works
This conservative heuristic looks for same-direction median changes across at least three consecutive bins, each containing at least three retained measurements. The total change must exceed the larger of 0.05 mag, three times the median reported uncertainty, or three times the median within-bin sample scatter. Gaps break a trend. It can miss real events, especially reversals, noisy curves, or sparse data, and can flag instrumental trends. It does not estimate statistical significance, confirm an eclipse, or forecast a recurrence. Results depend on bin size and quality filters.



Tracking Diagnostics
Reported sensor coordinates can reveal drift and sudden movement. A flagged change is a possible movement event; it does not prove a telescope slew or invalidate a measurement.

    The VStar button exports the current object and included runs, not the entire shortlist. Notes are stored in memory until downloaded; the notes JSON cannot yet restore a collection.
    

    Export and identity limitations
    Cross-run object links use a unique 3-arcsecond positional candidate, not a verified catalogue identity. Same names alone do not merge objects. Conflicting duplicate values and multiple detections at one epoch block VStar export. Ordinary and quality-filtered exports have been tested in VStar 2.25.0 on macOS; this is not an AAVSO submission file.
    VStar export keeps individual reported magnitudes, uncertainties and original exposure-start JD. No midpoint, HJD/BJD or passband conversion is invented. Run selection times are UTC from JD; ordinary chart labels retain recorded clocks.
    Seestar collection guide
    

    Seestar's Photometry/PhotoMetry folder contains named histories for selected stars and can retain older nights. In the examined capture, those histories included measurements from before the star's name appeared in the field CSVs.
    Subs folders contain per-exposure measurements for many stars, including rows explicitly labelled reference star. A field name such as Betelgeuse or Unknown is context, not an identification of the stars being measured.
    Target and field rows can be exports of the same observation. Exact overlap is excluded from the combined view rather than counted twice. Two seconds is the exposure duration; actual measurement times need not be two seconds apart.
    Reference roles and magnitudes are Seestar's reports. A reference label does not confirm a star's identity or constancy; the local D80 catalogue has positions/magnitudes, not names. External identification remains an explicit choice.
