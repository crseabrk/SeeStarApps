How variability screening works
This conservative heuristic looks for same-direction median changes across at least three consecutive bins, each containing at least three retained measurements. The total change must exceed the larger of 0.05 mag, three times the median reported uncertainty, or three times the median within-bin sample scatter. Gaps break a trend. It can miss real events, especially reversals, noisy curves, or sparse data, and can flag instrumental trends. It does not estimate statistical significance, confirm an eclipse, or forecast a recurrence. Results depend on bin size and quality filters.



Tracking Diagnostics
Reported sensor coordinates can reveal drift and sudden movement. A flagged change is a possible movement event; it does not prove a telescope slew or invalidate a measurement.
